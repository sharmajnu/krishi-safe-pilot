Title: TNAU Agritech Portal — Crop Production — Transplanted Hybrid Rice (Tamil Nadu)

Seed rate - 20 kg per hectare.
Nursery - Basal application of DAP at 2 kg/cent of nursery area. Sparse sowing of seeds at one kg/cent of nursery area will give robust seedlings with 1-2 tillers per seedling at the time of planting. If the soil is heavy, apply 4 kg gypsum/cent of nursery area, 10 days before pulling of seedlings.
Age of seedling - 20 to 25 days.
Spacing (cm) - 20 x 10 (50 hills/m2) or 25 x 10 (40 hills/m2) according to soil fertility.
Seedlings/hill - One (along with tillers if already produced).
Fertilizer - 175:60:60 kg N, P2O5 and K2O/ha.

Title: TNAU Agritech Portal — Dryland Agriculture — High Yield Groundnut (rainfed, Tamil Nadu)

Seed rate @ of 50 kg/acre is found to be sufficient. With the seed drill sowing 40 kg seed is sufficient.
Row to row spacing is 30 cm with the plant spacing of 10 cm.
Nitrogen 4 kg/acre (9 kg urea), 4 kg of phosphate (25 kg super phosphate) and 18 kg of ash (30 kg potash) to be applied as basal nutrient. [Note: "ash" appears to be a typo for K2O on the source page — interpretation, not verified text.]
Gypsum: For one acre 160 kg applied at 30-45 days after sowing in the sides, depending upon moisture of the soil.
Varieties: TMV 7 (widely cultivated across Tamil Nadu for the past 44 years), TMV 13 (red seeded, rich in oil content), VRI 2, VRI 3 (bunch type, yields in 95 days).
