Title: TNAU Expert System for Paddy — Rice Ecosystem (Transplanted Puddled Lowland Rice), Tamil Nadu

Nursery area: Select 20 cents (800 m2) of land area for raising seedlings for one hectare.
Seed rate: 30 kg for long duration, 40 kg for medium duration, 60 kg for short duration varieties and 20 kg for hybrids.

Optimum age of the seedlings is 18-22 days for short, 25-30 days for medium and 35-40 days for long duration varieties.

Spacing (cm): 15x10 (66 hills/m2) short duration; 20x10 (50 hills/m2) medium; 20x15 (33 hills/m2) long duration.
Transplant 2-3 seedlings/hill for short duration and 2 seedlings/hill for medium and long duration varieties.

Fertilizer blanket recommendation: 150:50:50 kg N:P2O5:K2O /ha.
Apply 25 kg of zinc sulphate with 50 kg sand just before transplanting.
Fill up the gaps between 7th and 10th DAT (days after transplanting).

Varieties: Ponmani (160-165 days, sow 1st-30th August); Co 43, IR20, ADT 38, ADT 39, Ponni, Improved White Ponni (125-135 days, sow 1st-30th September); ADT 36, ADT 37 (105-110 days).

SRI: Only 7-8 kg of seed is required to plant 1 ha.
